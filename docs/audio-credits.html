Sound credits

The overviews use edited recordings and device sounds to introduce scenes and connect them. The sounds are dramatizations of the fictional case. The narration and written evidence contain the case information.

All twelve source sounds are released under CC0 1.0. They are trimmed, balanced and hosted with this website. There is no background music.

You can preview each edited sound below. Its timing, fades and volume in the overview depend on the scene; effects become quieter while the narrator speaks.
