Sound and voice credits

The three overviews use recorded sound effects and AI voices. Both are dramatizations of a fictional case. The written documents hold the case information.

Voices

Each voice reads words that a case document quotes, and nothing else. The voices are stock voices from the open Kokoro text-to-speech model. None is a recording or a clone of a real person. There is no narrator.
